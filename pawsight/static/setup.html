      2Which bowl is the water bowl—
      
    
      
      The software cannot decide this: the bowls may stand only a few pixels apart,
      and the dog is much wider than that gap. The VLM tells drinking from eating;
      left/right is only a second opinion, and a disagreement is stored in the
      database, not hidden. A mistake here is harmless: you can come back and
      re-confirm at any time.
