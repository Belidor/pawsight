# Code of conduct

pawsight follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, criticise ideas rather than people, and
remember that everyone here is helping in their free time. Harassment,
insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or
discussions.

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/Belidor/pawsight/security/advisories/new)
or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove content and block
participants who break these rules.
